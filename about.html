Welcome to Secret Best Friend Quiz!

What would you do if you could ask your friends anything…and get completely honest answers? That’s the idea behind Secret Best Friend Quiz, a simple, nostalgic game we built “for fun” as part of a web-development project. Remember passing around paper quizzes in college to see who really knew you best? We wanted to capture that same excitement—only now it’s all online, instant, and anonymous.

How it works:

  1. Create your own quiz. Enter up to ten personal questions (multiple-choice or true/false) and pick the correct answers.
  2. Share your link. Send the generated URL to any friend—no sign-ups required.
  3. See their score. Your friends answer anonymously, and you’ll know who gets your quirks right…or hilariously wrong!

Built with core web technologies.

We wrote every line of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript ourselves—no frameworks—to show off fundamental client-side skills. Your quizzes and scores are stored right in your browser (using IndexedDB), so everything stays fast and private.

Why we made it.

Beyond a school assignment, Secret Best Friend Quiz is a celebration of simple, shared moments. It’s about laughter, nostalgia, and those “aha!” surprises when someone guesses your favorite snack or whether you’ve ever swum with sharks. We hope it brings you as much fun building and playing as we had coding it.

Give it a try—and see which of your friends really knows you best!
